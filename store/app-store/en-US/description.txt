Anchor+ is a fast, private note-taking app that works offline first. Your notes live on your phone, open instantly, and can be edited anywhere, with or without a connection. When you want them on every device, connect Anchor+ to your own self-hosted Anchor+ server and they sync in the background.

No ads. No tracking. No account with us. Open source under the AGPL.

WORKS WITHOUT A SERVER
• Start writing straight away: notes stay on the device
• Connect a server later and your notes move into your account when you sign in
• Back up every note, tag, picture and recording to a .zip file, and restore it

NOTES AND CHECKLISTS
• Notes and checklists with colours, tags, pinning and archiving
• Drag notes to arrange them; tick checklist items right from the list
• Rich text: headings, highlights in any colour and fonts from serif to handwriting
• Merge, copy, freeze or hide notes; delete with Undo instead of a confirmation
• Version history that shows what was added and removed

PICTURES, FILES AND RECORDINGS
• Take pictures, or several in a row, and place them right in the text
• Crop, mirror, caption and resize pictures
• Record voice notes, also while the phone is locked
• Attach any file, or a whole folder zipped
• Grab the text in a picture and copy it or insert it in a note
• Share text, links, pictures and files to Anchor+ from other apps

SEARCH
• Filter by colour, tag, pictures, checklists and reminders
• Regular expressions, case matching and a filter for notes that contain secrets
• With a server: search the text in your pictures (screenshots, receipts)

TOGETHER, WITH YOUR SERVER
• Sync across phones, the web and desktop
• Edit a note together, live, and see everyone's cursor
• Share a note with a public link
• Live images: put the phone's camera in a note for everyone who opens it

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Lock notes with Face ID or Touch ID
• Two-step sign-in with an authenticator app
• Your notes only ever go to the server you choose

MAKE IT YOURS
• Colour schemes such as Nord, Dracula, Solarized, Gruvbox, Catppuccin and Tokyo Night, an OLED black theme and custom themes
• Skeuomorphic, Neo-Brutalist and Lo-fi styles
• Reminders, a home screen widget and quick actions

Run your own server with Docker in a few minutes, on a home server, a Raspberry Pi or a small cloud machine. Instructions and source code: github.com/AlxndrJhn/anchor-plus
